Games you own but have not installed are found from Steam's local cache with no key. A key (steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey) switches to authoritative ownership and playtime. Stored locally only.

Read this before you use Steam Achiever

This tool changes achievement data on your real Steam account through the official Steamworks API. Unlocking achievements you did not earn is against Steam's Subscriber Agreement and many games' rules. By continuing you accept that:

  • Some game developers ban accounts for achievement or stat manipulation. This is separate from VAC and is per game. Rust is a known example.
  • Achievements can be wiped by games that validate them server-side.
  • VAC-secured games are blocked here, and the app closes itself if you launch one, but no automated safety net is perfect.
  • Your "now playing" status briefly changes to each game you open here.
  • You are doing this to your own account, at your own risk. The authors are not responsible for bans, lost progress, or other consequences.

Safest use: single-player and non-competitive games only. Do not run this while playing anything with anti-cheat.
